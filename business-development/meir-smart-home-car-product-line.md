# MDS Product Line Proposal for Meir - Smart Home and Smart Car Accessories

Date: 2026-06-09
Owner: Haim Daniel / Magen Daniel Systems LLP
Status: Initial business-development concept for supplier/product validation

## Purpose

This document captures the initial product-line strategy for approaching Meir / Digital Electric / Anak Electric with a new retail category.

The current decision is **not** to offer the MDS V2X product at this stage. The V2X system should remain for a later phase, after trust, distribution, and product-market fit are established.

The immediate opportunity is to source, manufacture, private-label, or export a set of practical smart products that Meir can sell through his existing electrical appliance store.

## Strategic Positioning

We should not offer Meir a single product. We should offer him a **complete new category**:

**MDS Smart Home + MDS Smart Car Accessories**

This category should complement his current business instead of competing with his existing products such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, ovens, TVs, and household appliances.

## Business Logic

The products should be selected based on the following criteria:

1. Easy to understand for retail customers.
2. Small or medium size, not heavy white goods.
3. Good gross-margin potential.
4. Simple installation or no installation.
5. Low warranty/service complexity.
6. Suitable for sourcing from India or other low-cost suppliers.
7. Not dependent on a specific vehicle model.
8. Not requiring immediate V2X integration.
9. Potential future connection to the MDS ecosystem/app.

## Important Regulatory Direction

Prefer products that are lower-risk from a certification and compliance perspective:

- Avoid large 230V appliances at this stage.
- Avoid products that require complex Israeli Standards Institute approval unless margin justifies it.
- Avoid products that modify vehicle ECU/CAN behavior.
- Prefer plug-and-play, battery-powered, USB-powered, solar-powered, 12V vehicle accessories, and Wi-Fi/BLE smart accessories.

Any product with Wi-Fi, BLE, RF, battery, 230V power supply, lithium battery, or safety-critical function still needs a compliance check before import/export.

## Future MDS Ecosystem Direction

The initial products can be sold as standalone retail products. However, the long-term MDS direction should keep the option open for future integration into an MDS app/ecosystem.

Possible future integration:

- Product QR code registration in MDS app
- Digital warranty
- User manuals
- Firmware/update support where applicable
- Local video storage access
- Optional cloud backup for security/dash camera events
- BLE/Wi-Fi device pairing
- Future connection to MDS Hub/V2X device

## Vehicle Product Architecture Principle

For vehicle accessories, avoid products that require compatibility per car model.

Prefer universal products:

- Dash cameras
- Tire inflators
- Jump starters
- Car vacuum cleaners
- TPMS kits
- USB-C car chargers
- Portable refrigerators
- Emergency warning lights

Avoid at first:

- Model-specific Android head units
- ECU/CAN-writing OBD products
- Products that may affect vehicle warranty
- Products requiring complex installation

## Camera / Recording Concept for Future MDS Vehicle Line

For vehicle cameras, the preferred future design is:

- Continuous local recording on microSD
- Loop recording with automatic overwrite after a defined period
- Event lock for accident, SOS, impact, violence, or manual trigger
- Optional phone/app download
- Optional cloud upload only for selected events or premium subscription

This avoids the cost of continuous cloud upload while still giving the customer useful evidence when needed.

## AI Future Concept - Incident Evidence Assistant

Future MDS AI can analyze data from:

- IMU/G-sensor
- GNSS/location/speed
- Camera evidence
- Manual SOS button

The AI should not determine legal fault. It should provide an evidence summary, for example:

- probable impact direction
- event time and location
- speed before event
- severity estimate
- relevant video clip
- suggested inspection area, such as front bumper, rear side, left side, right side

Recommended naming:

- MDS AI Incident Summary
- MDS Event Evidence Assistant

Avoid wording such as "fault decision" or "who is guilty".
